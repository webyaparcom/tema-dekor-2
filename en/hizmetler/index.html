Process

Handover in five steps

Move back and forth between the steps; durations are for an average flat.

  1. 01

    Step 1

    First meeting

    We listen to how you live at home; budget and timeline are written down from day one.

    schedule1 hour

  2. 02

    Step 2

    Measure and survey

    Every wall, socket and window is measured; existing furniture is listed.

    schedule1 day

  3. 03

    Step 3

    Concept board

    A two-option concept is presented with colour, fabric and wood samples.

    schedule2 weeks

  4. 04

    Step 4

    Site work

    We manage the trades; a short photo report arrives every week.

    schedule4–8 weeks

  5. 05

    Step 5

    Final touch

    Curtains, lamps and textiles go in; keys are handed over with care notes.

    schedule1 week
